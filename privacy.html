Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

A.R.C. (Art. Render. Create.) is an official student creative and media production club at the Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM), Kolkata. This website is a static showcase of our visual media, event coverage, and creative portfolio.

We do not require account registration, and we do not collect personal financial or sensitive private data. Information gathered is strictly limited to voluntary interactions (such as social recruitment queries or direct messages via Instagram).

2. Cookies and Local Storage

This website uses local browser storage solely to preserve your UI preferences (such as cookie consent acknowledgment). We do not deploy third-party cross-site behavioral tracking cookies.

3. External Links & Media

Our website features links to official social media channels including Instagram, YouTube, and event pages. When navigating to third-party platforms, please review their respective privacy policies as their data practices are governed independently.

4. Intellectual Property & Visual Rights

All photographs, aftermovies, 3D animations, and motion graphic assets presented on this site are intellectual property produced by A.R.C. members and respective collaborative institutional bodies. Unauthorized duplication or redistribution for commercial purposes is prohibited without written consent.

5. Contact Information

For questions regarding our privacy practices, media accreditation, or data inquiries, contact the A.R.C. team directly through our official social media handles or via IEM campus leadership.
